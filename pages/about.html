OUR STORY

Comfort Should Never Be a Compromise

ArthroStride began with a simple observation: many people live with foot, knee and back discomfort because everyday shoes were never designed for their feet.

We bring together arch support, cushioning and width options, and we take time to fit each customer properly, so the right pair feels right from the first step.

Our Mission

To make supportive footwear easy to understand, easy to choose and comfortable to wear all day.

  • Honest guidance, no pressure to buy
  • Fit first, style second, never one without the other
  • Clear help with prescriptions and insurance paperwork
