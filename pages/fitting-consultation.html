Ask a Fitter Now
01Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Fitting and advice are free, with zero obligation to buy anything.

02Do I need a prescription?

Not for a general fitting. For diabetic or therapeutic footwear insurance claims, bring your doctor's prescription.

03Can I walk in without booking?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booked slots are seen first, so booking saves you the wait.

04Can I reschedule?

Yes. Call +91 98765 43210 at least 3 hours before your slot.

05How long does a fitting take?

30 to 45 minutes: scan, walking check, matching and trial. No rushing.

06Will you fit my existing orthotics?

Yes. Bring them and we test them inside every pair you try.

07Can I use health insurance?

Many plans cover therapeutic footwear with a prescription. Bring your policy details and we will guide the paperwork.

08What if nothing fits well?

Then we say so. You leave with advice, not a pair that is wrong for your feet.
