FOOT CONDITIONS EXPLAINED

Know What Your Feet Need

A quick guide to what matters in a shoe for each condition.

Plantar Fasciitis

Pain under the heel or arch, often worst with the first steps of the day.

Look for

  • Deep heel cup
  • Firm arch support
  • Cushioned midsole

Better to avoid

Thin, flat soles and worn-out shoes.

Shop Plantar Fasciitis

Diabetic Footwear

Sensitive feet need protection from rubbing, pressure and irritation.

Look for

  • Seamless soft lining
  • Extra depth
  • Rocker sole

Better to avoid

Tight fits, hard seams and pointed toes.

Shop Diabetic Footwear

Wide Fit (2E / 4E)

Bunions, swelling or broad feet need room where the toes spread.

Look for

  • Wide anatomical toe box
  • Stretch upper
  • Adjustable closure

Better to avoid

Narrow or pointed toe shapes.

Shop Wide Fit (2E / 4E)

General information only, not medical advice. Please consult your doctor for diagnosis.

FAQ

Common Questions

How do I choose the right width?

Standard is 2E; choose 4E if you have bunions, swelling or use custom orthotics. Our fitting consultation measures both feet for free.

Can I use my own orthotics?

Yes. Every style has a removable footbed so your prescribed orthotic can be fitted.

Can I claim insurance?

Many therapeutic styles qualify with a doctor’s prescription. See the Insurance & Prescription page.

What is the exchange policy?

Unworn pairs can be exchanged within 30 days for a different size or width.
